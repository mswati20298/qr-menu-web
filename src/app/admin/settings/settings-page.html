Bill charges

Configure GST and service charge. These apply automatically to every customer order and show up as separate line items on the bill.

%

Standard GST for standalone restaurants in India is 5% (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST), with no input tax credit. Restaurants operating inside a hotel charge 18%.

%

Service charge is not a government tax — it's discretionary. Per CCPA (2022) guidelines it must never be forced on customers, and must be clearly disclosed as optional; customers can ask for it to be removed.

UPI payments

Customers see a UPI QR with the order amount on their order screen and pay straight into this account. When they tap "I've paid", the order shows as "UPI claimed" — check your UPI app, then mark it received. Leave empty to turn UPI payments off.
